Topic 01 — Section B

Strategies for Improving UX

⚡

Performance Optimization

Target under 3-second load times. Compress images, leverage CDNs, and implement lazy loading to reduce bounce rates dramatically.

📱

Mobile-First Design

Over 70% of e-commerce traffic is mobile. Design thumb-friendly interfaces with large tap targets and streamlined mobile checkout.

🧭

Intuitive Navigation

Clear categories, persistent search, breadcrumbs, and smart filtering help customers find products in under 3 clicks.

🤖

AI-Powered Search

Natural language search, typo tolerance, and visual search capabilities increase product discovery and conversion rates.

✅

Frictionless Checkout

Guest checkout, saved payment methods, one-click reorder, and multiple payment options reduce cart abandonment.

♿

Accessibility & Trust

WCAG compliance, clear trust badges, transparent pricing, and authentic reviews build confidence and widen your audience.

  • 5

Set Clear Response SLAs

Define and communicate service level agreements for each channel. Customers expect email within 24 hours, live chat under 2 minutes, and social media within 1 hour.

  • Email: respond within 24 hours, resolve within 48
  • Live chat: first response under 90 seconds
  • Social: acknowledge within 60 minutes
  • Track SLA compliance in your helpdesk dashboard
Topic 02 — Section C

Handling Complaints & Returns

📥

Receive

Acknowledge within SLA. Log all details in ticketing system.

→
🔍

Investigate

Review order history, verify issue, assess root cause thoroughly.

→
🤝

Empathize

Acknowledge feelings, own the problem, avoid blame-shifting.

→
🛠️

Resolve

Offer refund, replacement, or credit. Go beyond expectations.

→
🔄

Follow Up

Confirm satisfaction, prevent recurrence, update internal processes.

POLICY DESIGN

  • 30–60 day return windows
  • Free returns build confidence
  • Clear eligibility criteria
  • No-questions-asked for low-value items

PROCESS EFFICIENCY

  • Self-service return portals
  • Pre-printed return labels
  • Instant refund on drop-off
  • Real-time return tracking

TURNING IT AROUND

  • Offer exchanges over refunds
  • Loyalty points as compensation
  • Personalised win-back email
  • Collect feedback for improvement
Topic 03 — Section A

Loyalty Programs & Incentives

POPULAR
⭐

Points-Based Programs

Customers earn points per purchase, redeemable for discounts or free products. Simple to understand and gamify.

  • 1 point per $1 spent
  • Bonus points on birthdays
  • Redemption thresholds create urgency
  • Partner earn opportunities
HIGH VALUE
💎

Tiered Membership

Bronze → Silver → Gold status tiers with escalating benefits. Creates aspiration and increases spend-per-customer.

  • Free shipping at Silver tier
  • Early access at Gold level
  • Dedicated support for VIPs
  • Annual status challenges
GROWING
🌐

Referral & Community

Reward customers who bring others. Community programs build brand ambassadors and lower CAC significantly.

  • $10–$20 referral credits
  • UGC incentive campaigns
  • Private member communities
  • Affiliate program tiers
